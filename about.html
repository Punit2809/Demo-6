Our Story

Built to Close the Hiring Gap

Nevex Tech IT was founded on a simple frustration: exceptional technology professionals were being overlooked — not because they lacked skill, but because their resumes, outreach and interviews didn't reflect it.

We set out to change that. Today we combine expert coaching, a dedicated placement team and a written guarantee to give every professional a fair shot at the roles they've earned.

  • A dedicated career manager for every professional
  • Data-driven, U.S.-market resume optimization
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
